# Products

## What our candles are made of
- Soy and rapeseed wax blend, cotton wicks, and fragrance oils.
- All candles are vegan and are not tested on animals.
- They contain no paraffin and no lead.

- Medium and large candles can have a custom label (for example a name or a short message) for £4 extra.
- Custom candles take 3 extra working days to prepare and cannot be returned unless faulty.

## Candle care and safety
- On the first burn, let the wax melt all the way to the edge of the jar (about 2 to 3 hours). This prevents tunnelling.
- Trim the wick to about 5 mm before each use.
- Do not burn a candle for more than 4 hours at a time.
- Never leave a burning candle unattended, and keep it away from children, pets, and curtains.
- Stop using a candle when about 1 cm of wax is left.

## Allergies
- Fragrance oils can irritate sensitive skin or airways. A full ingredient list for each scent is on its product page.
- We cannot give medical advice. Customers with allergies should check the ingredient list or choose Unscented.
