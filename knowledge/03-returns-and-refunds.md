# Returns and refunds

## Changing your mind
- Unused, unlit candles in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- The customer pays the return shipping.
- Candles that have been lit cannot be returned unless they are defective.
- Gift cards and personalized (custom label) candles cannot be returned.

## Damaged or defective items
- If a candle arrives broken, or is defective (for example the wick will not stay lit, or it tunnels badly), we replace it or refund it at no cost to the customer.
- We need the order number and a photo of the problem, sent within 14 days of delivery.
- The customer does not need to send a damaged item back.

## How refunds work
- Refunds go back to the original payment method.
- We process a refund within 3 business days of receiving the return (or of approving a damage claim).
- The bank or card company can then take up to 5 more business days to show it.
- Original shipping costs are only refunded when the item was damaged, defective, or we sent the wrong thing.

## How to start a return
- Reply to this inbox with the order number and the reason. The team sends back the return address and instructions.
