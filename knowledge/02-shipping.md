# Shipping

## Where we ship
- We ship to the United Kingdom and the European Union only.
- We do not ship to the United States, Canada, Australia, or anywhere else yet.

## How long it takes
- Orders are packed and sent within 2 working days of payment.
- UK delivery: 2 to 4 working days after dispatch.
- EU delivery: 5 to 10 working days after dispatch.
- We do not offer next-day or express delivery.

## What it costs
- UK: £3.95, or free on orders of £40 or more.
- EU: £9.95, with no free shipping threshold.
- EU customers may have to pay import duties or taxes on delivery. These are set by the customer's country and are not included in our prices.

## Tracking
- Every order gets a tracking link by email when it is dispatched.
- If the dispatch email has not arrived 3 working days after ordering, the customer should check their spam folder, then contact us with their order number.

## Late or missing parcels
- A UK parcel counts as late after 7 working days from dispatch; an EU parcel after 15 working days.
- For a late parcel we need the order number. The team then opens a trace with the courier, which takes up to 5 working days.
- If the courier confirms the parcel is lost, we send a replacement or refund, whichever the customer prefers.
