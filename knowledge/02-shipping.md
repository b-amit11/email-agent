# Shipping

## Where we ship
- We ship to all 50 US states and to Canada only.
- We do not ship to Mexico, the United Kingdom, Europe, Australia, or anywhere else yet.

## How long it takes
- Orders are packed and shipped within 2 business days of payment.
- US delivery: 3 to 5 business days after the order ships.
- Canada delivery: 7 to 12 business days after the order ships.
- We do not offer overnight or express delivery.

## What it costs
- United States: $5.95, or free on orders of $50 or more.
- Canada: $14.95, with no free shipping threshold.
- Canadian customers may have to pay import duties or taxes on delivery. These are set by Canada and are not included in our prices.

## Tracking
- Every order gets a tracking link by email when it ships.
- If the shipping email has not arrived 3 business days after ordering, the customer should check their spam folder, then contact us with their order number.

## Late or missing packages
- A US package counts as late after 8 business days from the day it shipped; a Canadian package after 15 business days.
- For a late package we need the order number. The team then opens a trace with the carrier, which takes up to 5 business days.
- If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we send a replacement or refund, whichever the customer prefers.
