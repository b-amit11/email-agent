# Orders and payment

## Order numbers
- Order numbers look like MF-12345 (the letters MF, a dash, and five digits).
- The order number is in the confirmation email sent right after checkout.

## Changing or cancelling an order
- An order can be changed or cancelled only before it has been dispatched.
- Because we dispatch within 2 working days, customers should email as soon as possible with their order number.
- Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled; the customer can return it instead under the returns policy.
- Only a team member can confirm whether a change or cancellation was possible.

## Payment
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.
- We do not accept bank transfers, cheques, or cash.
- All prices are in pounds sterling (GBP) and include UK VAT.

## Discount codes
- One discount code can be used per order.
- A code cannot be added after an order has been placed.
- New customers get 10% off their first order by signing up to the newsletter on the website.

## Gift cards
- Digital gift cards are available from £10 to £100 and are emailed straight away.
- Gift cards are valid for 24 months and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Wholesale
- We do not have a wholesale or bulk-discount programme at the moment.
